Privacy Policy

VexaKit does not require an account. Tools designed for local processing handle selected text and files within your browser. Your browser may store preferences such as theme, recent tools, favourites and language selection in local storage.

Third-party resources

The site may request resources from third parties, including Google Fonts and a QR-code library hosted by a CDN. Those providers may receive standard connection data such as your IP address and browser information under their own policies.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics or advertising service is configured in this package. If you add one, update this policy to explain the provider, data collected, purpose and opt-out choices.

Your choices

You can clear this site's browser storage using your browser settings. Do not upload or enter confidential information unless you have assessed the tool and your browser environment.
